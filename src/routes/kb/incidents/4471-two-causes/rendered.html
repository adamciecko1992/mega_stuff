#4471 · three problems, not one

owner support desk resolved reviewed 2026-10-28 · 0 days ago

Ticket #4471 holds 412 reports about dates. It was merged as one issue by subject line (decision 0001), but it covered three different problems. Each got its own ticket, and all three are closed.

The three problems

problemreportstenantswhat happens
parser 374 11 The shared parser reads every date month first. Day-first tenants get a flipped month: a whole different date, not a day's difference.
hint 33 3 The booking-v3 form shows MM/DD/YYYY to day-first tenants, and people type what the hint says.
not the parser bug 5 5 A one-day or timezone shift, not a month/day swap. Merged by subject line, not by cause.

The parser and hint problems are both month/day confusion, told apart by which part of the system misreads the date. The third group was never that: it was swept into #4471 because its subject lines matched, not because its cause did.

What was really in the "not the parser bug" group

  • kettle.books: invite said Tuesday 12 May, calendar said Monday 11 May — one day, same month.
  • alder.physio: every June booking is one day off, only in June.
  • pale.harbour: the same meeting showed Tuesday in Lisbon and Monday in Toronto.
  • lantern.row: Tuesday 6 October showed as Monday.
  • nine.lanterns: the invite arrived before the booking was made.

A month/day swap turns 12 May into 5 December — a different date, not a day's difference. Four of these five move by exactly one day or depend on timezone, which points to a DST or timezone boundary bug, not the parser. The fifth has no date symptom at all and needs its own look.

What happened

  • The parser cause stayed on #4471. Its own ticket here tracks it; closed.
  • The hint cause got its own ticket, separate from #4471. It affects booking-v3 forms in de-DE, pt-PT and es-ES; closed.
  • The 5 misfiled reports got their own ticket, pulled out of the parser count; closed.

Notes

  • Intake tags are a guess from the reporter's browser. In the parser group, 23 reports were tagged hint; in the hint group, 15 were tagged parser. Do not sort by intake tag.
  • The dedupe rule merges on subject line alone (decision 0001). That is fast, but it is why a timezone bug and a parser bug can end up under the same ticket: nobody reads the body until someone has to split it back out.
